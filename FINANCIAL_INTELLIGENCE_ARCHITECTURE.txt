1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================
Nexora / DocuMind does not rely on a single generic AI prompt or a rigid keyword
list. Instead, it utilizes a multi-tier hybrid architecture combining:
  1. Classical Supervised Machine Learning (Scikit-Learn TF-IDF classification)
  2. Rule-based Domain Heuristics & Deterministic Regular Expressions
  3. Unsupervised Machine Learning (Isolation Forest Anomaly Detection)
  4. Vector-based Similarity (Cosine Similarity for Duplicate Identification)
  5. State-of-the-art Large Language Models (Groq LLaMA-3.3-70B)
  6. Strict Accounting Reconciliation Rules (Deterministic Math Verification)

This hybrid design guarantees high processing speed, zero hallucination for
mathematical auditing, resilience against poor-quality OCR, and deep contextual
understanding.

2. DOCUMENT CLASSIFICATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
Documents are classified through a 3-tier arbitration hierarchy:

  Uploaded File
       │
       ▼
  [OCR Engine: Tesseract + PDFPlumber] -> Cleaned Text
       │
       ▼
  [TIER 1: Trained ML Classifier (classifier.joblib)]
       │
       ├── If Confidence >= 0.50 ───> Accepted Primary Category
       │
       └── If Confidence < 0.50 or Ambiguous
             │
             ▼
  [TIER 2: Financial Heuristic Classifier (heuristics.py)]
       │
       ├── Trigger Patterns & Weighted Term Frequency Analysis
       │   (Assigns 70% - 85% calibrated confidence)
       │
       ▼
  [TIER 3: AI Semantic Arbitration (Groq LLaMA-3.3-70B)]
       │
       └── Reconciles edge cases, verifies layout, and updates the database.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1. TIER 1: CLASSICAL MACHINE LEARNING MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: backend/app/ml/artifacts/classification/classifier.joblib
- Algorithm: Scikit-Learn TF-IDF Vectorizer + SGDClassifier / LogisticRegression
- How it works:
    1. The cleaned OCR text is converted into word n-grams (1-gram and 2-gram).
    2. Words are assigned TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency)
       weights, down-weighting common English stop words while highlighting
       informative words like "invoice", "subtotal", "purchase", "order".
    3. The classifier calculates a probability distribution across known classes.
    4. If the top class score exceeds the threshold (0.50), it is adopted.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2. TIER 2: FINANCIAL HEURISTIC CLASSIFIER (heuristics.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: backend/app/ml/classification/heuristics.py
- Why it is needed:
    Scanned business documents often contain OCR noise, non-standard layouts,
    or specialized terminology that classical models may misclassify as
    "unclassified".
- Mechanism:
    Evaluates documents using two scoring components:
      a) Primary Trigger Patterns (Weight: 3.0): Exact multi-word regex phrases
         unique to a specific document type.
      b) Secondary Vocabulary (Weight: 1.0): Contextual words frequently present.
- Calibrated Confidence Formula:
    Score = min(0.85, 0.60 + (matched_score / 15.0) * 0.25)
    Guarantees reliable, deterministic routing with 70% to 85% confidence.

- Supported Financial Categories:
    1. INVOICE:
       - Triggers: "tax invoice", "commercial invoice", "gstin", "bill to"
       - Terms: subtotal, gst, cgst, sgst, igst, amount due, invoice date
    2. BANK STATEMENT:
       - Triggers: "statement of account", "account summary", "opening balance",
                   "closing balance", "ifsc code"
       - Terms: withdrawal, deposit, credit, debit, cheque, clearing, transaction
    3. SALARY SLIP / PAYSLIP:
       - Triggers: "payslip for the month", "salary slip", "earnings and deductions",
                   "basic pay", "net pay"
       - Terms: hra, provident fund, pf, gratuity, esi, deductions, gross salary
    4. UTILITY BILL:
       - Triggers: "electricity bill", "water bill", "consumer number", "meter number",
                   "units consumed", "kwh"
       - Terms: tariff, sanctioned load, billing period, due date, power distribution
    5. RECEIPT:
       - Triggers: "payment receipt", "cash memo", "pos terminal", "receipt no"
       - Terms: change due, cash, card, cashier, tender, store #
    6. PURCHASE ORDER:
       - Triggers: "purchase order", "po number", "order confirmation", "vendor order"
       - Terms: ship to, deliver to, requisition, payment terms, buyer
    7. TAX DOCUMENT:
       - Triggers: "form 16", "form 26as", "income tax department", "pan card",
                   "assessment year"
       - Terms: tds, tan, taxable income, rebate, financial year
    8. INSURANCE POLICY / CLAIM:
       - Triggers: "insurance policy", "policy schedule", "sum insured",
                   "premium receipt"
       - Terms: policyholder, deductible, coverage, nominee, insured
    9. CREDIT NOTE / DEBIT NOTE:
       - Triggers: "credit note", "debit note", "adjustment voucher"
       - Terms: against invoice, credit memo, debit memo, adjustment
    10. GENERAL FINANCIAL STATEMENT:
       - Triggers: "balance sheet", "profit and loss", "income statement",
                   "cash flow statement"
       - Terms: assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, ebitda, depreciation

3. FINANCIAL TERMINOLOGY UNDERSTANDING & FIELD EXTRACTION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC REGEX & CONTEXT-WINDOW EXTRACTORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: backend/app/ml/extraction/financial_extractors.py & invoice_extractor.py
- How it works:
    Instead of assuming every document is an invoice, the system instantiates a
    specialized extractor registered in ExtractionRegistry:

    - BankStatementExtractor:
        * Account Number: captures 9-18 digit account strings following "A/c No",
          "Account #", "Account Number".
        * IFSC Code: validates RBI format `^[A-Z]{4}0[A-Z0-9]{6}$`.
        * Balance Endpoints: regex anchors targeting "Opening Balance",
          "Closing Balance", "Total Debits / Withdrawals", "Total Credits / Deposits".
    - SalarySlipExtractor:
        * Employee Identifier: captures employee IDs (e.g., EMP-10492, Staff No).
        * Earnings & Deductions: extracts Basic Salary, Gross Earnings, Total
          Deductions, and Net Take-Home Pay.
    - UtilityBillExtractor:
        * Consumer & Meter Number: parses consumer IDs and electricity meter codes.
        * Energy Units: parses "kWh" or "Units Consumed" and total bill amount.
    - ReceiptExtractor & InvoiceExtractor:
        * Identifies Vendor, Bill To, Subtotal, Taxes (CGST/SGST/IGST), Total.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2. FINANCIAL CURRENCY & NUMBER NORMALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: backend/app/ml/extraction/normalizers.py
- Financial values appear in various formats across global and Indian ledgers:
    * ₹ 1,50,000.00
    * Rs. 150000.50
    * $1,250.00
    * 1.250,00 EUR
- The normalization engine removes currency prefixes, strips localized comma
  separators, and parses figures into Python `Decimal` instances with fixed
  precision (avoiding IEEE-754 floating-point rounding errors).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3. GENERATIVE AI DEEP SEMANTIC ENTITY EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Model: LLaMA-3.3-70B-Versatile via Groq API
- The AI service sends a structured prompt enforcing strict JSON output:
    * Identified Entities: Vendor, Buyer, Bank, Account, Tax IDs.
    * Line Items Array: Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Amount.
    * Financial Breakdown: Currency, Subtotal, Tax/GST, Discounts, Grand Total.
    * Risk Assessment Narrative: Human-like explanation of inconsistencies,
      irregular tax percentages, or unexpected clauses.

5. DOMAIN VALIDATION & MATHEMATICAL RECONCILIATION RULES
================================================================================
The platform distinguishes itself by not relying on AI guesses for numbers.
All extracted figures must satisfy strict deterministic domain math:

1. INVOICE RECONCILIATION:
   Formula:  Subtotal + Tax (or GST) ≈ Total Amount  (Tolerance: ₹0.05)
   Rule:     Date Consistency (Due Date >= Invoice Date)

2. BANK STATEMENT RECONCILIATION:
   Formula:  Opening Balance + Total Deposits - Total Withdrawals == Closing Balance
   Rule:     Must contain valid Account Number and non-empty balance endpoints.

3. SALARY SLIP RECONCILIATION:
   Formula:  Gross Salary - Total Deductions == Net Pay
   Rule:     Must contain valid Employee Identifier and non-negative pay figures.

4. UTILITY BILL AUDIT:
   Rule:     Consumer Number validation, positive charge verification, and
             past vs. future due date consistency.

5. ISOLATION FOREST STATISTICAL AUDIT:
   Evaluates a feature vector:
     [Total Amount, Line Item Count, Character Count, Tax-to-Total Ratio]
   Assigns an Anomaly Score (< 0.0 indicates statistical outlier).
